Subagents from a parallel run stay on the bar, the menu-bar icon closes when you click away,
and the project has a licence.

**A parallel run shows its subagents.** An agent launched to run in the background outlives
the turn that started it, but the badge counting it was cleared at the end of that turn — it
appeared for about a second and a half and then vanished, however long the agent kept
working. The badge now lasts as long as the subagent does, whether it runs in the foreground
or the background.

**The menu-bar popover closes on a click outside.** On macOS it stayed open until you pressed
the icon again.

**The menu-bar icon reads on a light menu bar.** Idle and empty dots were painted in near
white straight onto the bar; a fill too close to the bar behind it now gets an outline,
keeping the colour you picked. Blocked also draws as a triangle and error as a square, so the
two states you act on are distinct where the icon has no room to pulse.

**AgentStatus is MIT-licensed.** The repository had no licence before, which left everyone
with the right to read it and nothing else — building, redistributing, or contributing is
allowed now.

macOS 13 or later (universal, Apple Silicon and Intel), or Windows 11 on x64.
